# Saved evidence

These are anonymized copies of saved results. Responses, scores and selection results are preserved. Identifier redactions and dependent checksum changes are documented in [anonymization](../../docs/ANONYMIZATION.md).

- a10-publication.json contains all 41 original display rows, responses, scores and selection.
- a05-publication.json, a06-publication.json and a09-publication.json retain earlier comparisons, including no-selection outcomes.
- a10-terminal-audit.json records the completed independent audit.
- a10-request.json records the exact old request. It is evidence, not authority to repeat it.
- history/A04.md through history/A10.md preserve the original terminal reports.

The copied publication has an integrity-checked identity. The full original token/state traces and 384-file delivery are still in the canonical project. They are not all duplicated here. Consequently, the new history reader can check publication bytes and rescore saved responses; it does not claim to rerun the full mechanical audit in this folder.

The word publication in these historical filenames means a saved display artifact. The completed audits establish its research status. This copy contains no credentials or model weights; the original research records remain in the private archive.
